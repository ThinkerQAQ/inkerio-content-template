# Photography Exposure Essentials

Exposure is about more than brightness. Aperture, shutter speed, and ISO also affect depth of field, the appearance of motion, and image noise.

## Aperture

A wider aperture (a lower f-number) generally admits more light and produces a shallower depth of field. It can help separate the subject from the background.

## Shutter Speed

A faster shutter speed freezes motion but lets in light for less time. Slower exposures can convey movement, though camera shake becomes more noticeable.

## ISO

When aperture and shutter speed are fixed, increasing ISO can brighten the recorded image, but may also introduce noise and reduce dynamic range. The best setting depends on the camera and the scene.
